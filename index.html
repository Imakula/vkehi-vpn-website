Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about VKEHI VPN, Sri Lanka ISP bypasses and protocols.

Does this VPN consume my main standard internet quota?
▼
No! VKEHI VPN routes through your ISP's specialized unlimited package (e.g. Dialog Zoom 724, Dialog TikTok, Airtel Zoom 215, SLT Netflix, Mobitel Unlimited). Your standard daytime/nighttime quota remains completely untouched.
What is the difference between SG IP and LK IP?
▼
SG IP (Singapore) is the default, ultra-fast route optimized for international browsing, 4K streaming, downloads, and low latency international gaming.

LK IP (Sri Lanka) retains a Sri Lankan residential IP address while tunneling through restrictions, perfect for local banking apps, government portals, and local services that block foreign IPs.
How fast is the setup process after purchasing?
▼
Once you submit your order and payment receipt, our team verifies and delivers your V2Ray config link directly to your customer dashboard and via WhatsApp / Telegram within 10 to 30 minutes.
Can I use my subscription on multiple devices?
▼
Yes! Depending on your chosen tier: Starter supports 1 device, Standard supports 2 simultaneous devices, Pro Gamer supports 3 devices, and Unlimited VIP supports up to 5 simultaneous devices.
Which client app do you recommend for Android and Windows?
▼
For Android, we recommend NetMod VPN Client or V2Box. For Windows PC, we recommend NetMod PC or v2rayN. All configs are 100% compatible.
